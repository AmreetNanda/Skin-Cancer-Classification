Common Skin Diseases We Detect

Vascular lesions

Symptoms: Red/purple spots, visible blood vessels.

Cause: Fragile blood vessels or injury.

Treatment: Laser therapy, sclerotherapy.

Actinic keratoses

Symptoms: Rough, scaly patches.

Cause: UV exposure, sun damage.

Treatment: Cryotherapy, topical medication.

Basal cell carcinoma

Symptoms: Pearly bumps, open sores.

Cause: UV-induced DNA damage.

Treatment: Surgical removal, Mohs surgery.

Benign keratosis-like lesions

Symptoms: Waxy, wart-like growths.

Cause: Non-cancerous skin changes.

Treatment: Cosmetic removal if desired.

Dermatofibroma

Symptoms: Firm, small nodules.

Cause: Minor skin injuries.

Treatment: None unless symptomatic.

Melanocytic nevi

Symptoms: Dark, round/oval spots (moles).

Cause: Accumulation of melanocytes.

Treatment: Monitor; remove if necessary.

Melanoma

Symptoms: Irregular dark moles, bleeding spots.

Cause: UV damage, genetics.

Treatment: Surgery, immunotherapy, radiation.
